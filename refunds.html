Refunds and Disputes

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Membership

  • Your first 30 days are free, so there is nothing to refund.
  • The yearly membership fee of ₦20,000 is not refundable once your membership is active, except when we charged you by mistake, charged you twice, or the law requires a refund. If that happens, contact us within 7 days and we will put it right.

2. Buying and selling on LBN (protected payment)

  • Chatting and making offers are free. You can cancel a trade at any time before payment, at no cost.
  • When the buyer pays, LBN holds the money. It is released to the seller only after both people confirm the trade is complete, or after the LBN team decides a dispute in the seller's favour.
  • LBN's fee is charged only when a payment is made, and it is not charged on trades that end in a full refund.
  • The payment provider's own processing charge on a refunded payment may not be recoverable. LBN refunds the buyer the full agreed amount that was paid.

3. When can a buyer get a refund?

  • The seller did not deliver by the agreed delivery date, or at all.
  • What arrived was not as described in the post or the agreed offer.
  • The goods or service were poor quality or incomplete in a way that was not agreed.
  • The seller stopped replying after being paid.

A refund is not guaranteed. The LBN team decides each dispute from the chat and the evidence.

4. How to open a dispute

  1. Open the trade chat and tap Open a dispute. This button appears once a deal is agreed and before the trade is completed.
  2. Choose what went wrong and explain it clearly.
  3. Add evidence: photos, screenshots, receipts or a PDF. Both people can add evidence while the dispute is open.
  4. The money stays on hold. The LBN team is alerted straight away, reads the chat and the evidence, and may message you both. We aim to review disputes quickly, usually within a few working days.
  5. The team decides to release the money to the seller, refund the buyer in full, or split it. Both people see the decision and the reason.

Keep all communication inside the LBN trade chat. Messages and agreements made outside LBN cannot be used as evidence, and money paid outside LBN is not protected.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Paystack. After we approve a refund, banks and card issuers usually take several working days to show it.

6. Handouts and books

Handouts and books are digital files you can download straight away, so purchases are final. If a file is broken, empty or clearly not what was described, contact us within 7 days and we will fix it or refund you.

7. Fair use

Opening false disputes, sending false evidence, or taking payment and not delivering can lead to suspension or removal from LBN, and we may share information with the authorities where the law requires.

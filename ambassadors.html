LBN Ambassador Recruitment

Represent Lagos Business Network at your school or business community.

What ambassadors receive

  • A monthly stipend of ₦70,000
  • An extra ₦500 for every person you bring in who buys a subscription
  • An Ambassador badge on your profile
  • An official certificate you can add to your CV
  • An official 12-month appointment

How it works

  1. Apply using the form. You need a referee who can vouch for you.
  2. Our team screens every application. Applications are subject to screening and final management approval.
  3. If you are selected you start a two-week trial.
  4. After a successful trial and final approval you become a confirmed ambassador for 12 months.
  5. Appointments are never renewed automatically. Every renewal depends on review and performance.

Each participating school has up to 15 places: 6 Business Owners, 5 Students and 4 Lecturers.

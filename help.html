How to use LBN

Simple steps. Tap a question to open it.

1. How do I join?

Tap Log in, then Create an account. Type your name, choose if you are a student or a lecturer, then your email and a password. Lecturers also type their school or university.

2. How do I pay my membership?

After you log in, tap Pay. You pay ₦20,000 for one year by card, bank transfer or USSD. The payment is safe and handled by Paystack.

3. How do I learn?

Tap Classes. Pick a lecturer, open their class, and watch the video lessons. Tap Live classes to join a lecturer live. At the time of the class, tap Join live class.

4. When can I use the marketplace?

Students unlock the marketplace when they graduate. You do not have to wait a full year. In each class, tap Mark as complete after every lesson. Your lecturers record when you pass an assessment. When you have enough of both, tap Ask to graduate on your dashboard and the LBN team reviews your evidence. Being active helps you learn faster, but activity alone does not graduate you. Lecturers unlock it when the LBN team has checked that they are real.

5. How do I show what I sell?

Open Market, tap Post something, write about your product or service, add a price, and add a photo or a video from your phone. Then tap Post.

6. I made a mistake in my post or comment

Tap Edit under your post or comment, fix it, and tap Save. To change a photo or video, tap Delete and post again.

7. How do I follow someone?

Tap Follow next to their name. Tap their name to see their page, with their posts, followers and following. Tap Share profile on your own page to send your link to anyone.

8. How do I buy or order something safely?

Tap Message seller on their post. Agree the price in the chat by sending an offer. When the seller accepts, tap Pay now. LBN holds your money. When you get exactly what you ordered, tap Confirm and the seller is paid. If it is not right, tap Open a dispute and the money stays on hold until the LBN team decides.

9. How do I stay safe?

Never send money outside LBN. Never share bank details, card numbers or OTP codes. LBN staff will never ask for your password. If someone asks you to, tap Report.

Where are my notifications and how do I log out?

Tap the bell at the top for notifications. Tap the three lines at the top for Settings and activity, where you can edit your profile, see your orders and log out.

How do I buy a handout or book?

Open Handouts and books from the Me menu. Tap Buy on the one you want and pay with Paystack. Your file appears in My purchases and you can download it any time. The lecturer is told who bought it.

10. I need help

Tap Help • Contact us and send us a message. We will reply to your email.

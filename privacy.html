Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Lagos Business Network ("LBN") is responsible for your personal information on this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, role, school or company name, and your password (stored in scrambled form, never readable).
  • Profile details: your photo, bio, followers, who you follow, and the language you choose.
  • Private contact details: a phone number or contact email, only if you add them.
  • What you post: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, reposts, tags, and messages you send.
  • Trades and payments: trade chats, offers, agreed amounts, fees, dispute evidence you upload, payment references from Paystack, and the bank account you give us for payouts. We do not see or store your card number.
  • Learning: lessons you complete, assessments, graduation requests, and handouts you buy.
  • Usage and safety: profile visits and post views (used for the analytics members see), notifications, and information used to stop repeat free trials, such as a scrambled form of your email, an anonymous device code and network address.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account, membership, marketplace, trades, lessons and messages. This is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • To take payments, hold money safely, pay sellers and refund buyers, and to keep records the law requires.
  • To keep LBN safe: stopping scams, fake accounts and repeat free trials, and settling disputes.
  • To tell you about your account, trades, payments and membership.
  • To show members their own business analytics.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertisements.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other members can see your profile and posts as your privacy settings allow. Your phone number and contact email are hidden unless you choose to share them.
  • The LBN team. Only admins who need it for their job can see more, for example the team that handles disputes and payments. We keep a log of changes to the admin team.
  • Service providers that help us run LBN: Paystack (payments) and Supabase (secure hosting and database). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • The law. We may share information when a court, regulator or the police legally require it.

5. Your choices and rights

In Settings, Account and privacy, you can: change your information, choose who can see your profile, message you, tag you and comment, choose who sees your phone and contact details, mute notifications, block people, download a copy of your data, deactivate your account, or delete it. You also have the right to ask us to correct your information, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. To use a right that is not in settings, contact us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

6. How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your profile details, posts, comments, followers, messages you sent and uploaded files, and close your login. We keep records of trades, payments and disputes for accounting and legal reasons. They show "Deleted member".
  • We keep a scrambled record that a free trial was used, so it cannot be claimed again.
  • Backups are removed on a regular cycle.

7. Keeping it safe

Connections are encrypted. Private information is stored in tables that only the account owner, and admins with the right permission, can read. Passwords are never stored in plain form. No system is perfectly safe, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

8. Children

LBN is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Information stored abroad

Our hosting and payment providers may store or process information outside Nigeria. We choose providers that protect information with appropriate safeguards.

10. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you logged in, remember your language and small settings, and make the app work offline. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make an important change, we will tell members on the website.

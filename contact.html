Common questions

How much is the membership?

Membership is ₦20,000 per year, paid securely through Paystack. It gives you classes, handouts and books, and access to the marketplace once you qualify.

I forgot my password

On the log in page, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We will send you a link to set a new one. If you are logged in, use Change password on your dashboard.

Why is the marketplace locked for me?

Students unlock the marketplace after finishing the one-year program. Lecturers unlock it once their account is verified as a real teacher from a school or university.

How do I become a verified lecturer?

Sign up as a lecturer and enter your school or university. The LBN team checks that you are a real lecturer, then approves your account.

How do I report a scam?

Inside a trade chat, tap Report a problem. You can also send us a message above and choose Report a scam. Never send money outside LBN and never share bank details, card numbers or OTP codes.

Can I cancel a trade?

Yes. Either side can cancel from the trade chat before any payment has been made.

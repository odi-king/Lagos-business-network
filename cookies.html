Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. This is a plain-language draft and is still to be reviewed by a lawyer.

1. What this page covers

Cookies and similar browser storage are small pieces of information saved on your phone or computer. This page lists what Lagos Business Network (LBN) saves, why, and how you control it.

2. What we save (essential)

These are needed for LBN to work. You cannot switch them off here, but you can clear them in your browser at any time (you will be logged out).

  • Login session (Supabase): keeps you logged in and keeps your account safe.
  • Language and settings: remembers your language and small choices such as closed banners and reminders.
  • Device key: a random code that helps us stop spam and handle feedback on one device.
  • Offline use: lets the app open faster and work with a weak connection.
  • Payment progress: remembers a payment you have started so we can finish it if the page closes.

3. Security check (Cloudflare Turnstile)

On the login, sign-up and password forms we use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell real people from automated attacks. Cloudflare may set a cookie or read information about your browser for this purpose only. It is strictly necessary for security and is not used for advertising.

4. Services that load when you use them

  • Paystack: opens when you pay. It has its own privacy and cookie rules.
  • LiveKit: carries live video and audio in classes, streams and meetings.

5. What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not sell information about you, and we do not use website statistics or tracking tools today. If we ever add an optional tool, it will stay off until you say yes on this page.

6. Your choice for optional tools

Optional tools are currently none, so there is nothing to switch on. Your choice below is saved on this device for the future.

7. How to clear it

In your browser settings, choose "Clear browsing data" or "Clear site data" for this website. You will be logged out and your small settings will reset.

Protected trading on LBN
  • Make payments only through the Pay now button in this trade chat. LBN verifies payments and protects eligible trades through its dispute process.
  • Never send money directly to a seller's personal bank account.
  • Never share phone numbers, bank details, card numbers or OTP codes.
  • Keep negotiations, delivery agreements and trade communication inside LBN.
  • If someone requests payment outside LBN or behaves suspiciously, tap Report a problem.

LBN transaction fee

Standard transactions: 1%. Large transactions: 0.5%. The applicable fee and seller payout must be displayed before payment.

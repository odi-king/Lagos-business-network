Join the Monitoring Department

The independent team that checks the work of LBN ambassadors and verifies real performance.

Roles

  • Head of Ambassador Monitoring
  • Performance Monitoring Officers
  • Field Verification Officers
  • Data and Fraud Analysts
  • Member Verification Officers

Who can apply

  • Qualified graduates
  • NYSC members
  • Experienced professionals
  • Trained field personnel

The team is separate from the ambassador programme. Ambassadors cannot join it, so the team stays independent of the people it checks. Applications are reviewed by management.

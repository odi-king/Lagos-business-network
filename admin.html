Graduation requests

Students ask to graduate only when they have enough proven lectures, passed assessments and own the required materials. Read the evidence, then approve or decline.

Graduation requirements

A lecture is a live class attended with the code, or an uploaded video watched to the end. A day counts only if the student had lectures from enough different lecturers that day.

Required books and handouts

Graduation certificates

Find a certificate by name, email or number. Anyone can check a number on graduation-certificate.html?no=NUMBER. Withdrawing one needs a reason.

Graduation gift packages

A package is created when a graduation is approved. Mark it Shipped with the courier and tracking number, then Delivered. The graduate is notified each time.

Membership plans and payments

Nigeria pays in naira through Paystack. Other countries pay in dollars. NGN and USD are never added together. Members cancel any time and keep access until their paid time ends.

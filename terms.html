  • Each person may have one account. Creating extra accounts to get more free trials is not allowed.

Lecturers and businesses must be approved by the LBN team before they get their badge and marketplace access. Students reach the marketplace after the LBN team confirms they have graduated, through our learning requirements.

3. Membership and the free month

  • Every new member gets 30 days of free access, counted from the day they register. Each person can have one free month.
  • After that, membership costs ₦20,000 for one year. Paying adds 12 months after your current end date, so paying early never wastes time.

  • When your membership ends, you can still log in, but you lose access to member features until you renew.

4. Posting, selling and buying

  • Posts about products, services and needs must be honest, lawful and about something you really have or want. Prices and descriptions must be accurate.
  • You may not sell or ask for anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or fraudulent, or anything that breaks Nigerian law.
  • To keep buyers and sellers safe, phone numbers, bank details and links are not allowed in posts, comments and chats. Deals are agreed in the LBN trade chat. Do not ask anyone to pay or deliver outside LBN.
  • We may remove posts and suspend accounts that break these rules.

5. Trades and protected payment

  • A trade starts when a buyer and a seller chat on a post. Chatting, making offers and negotiating are free.
  • When a deal is agreed, the buyer pays through LBN. LBN holds the money until the seller has delivered and both people confirm that the transaction is complete and they are satisfied, or until the LBN team settles a dispute.
  • LBN charges a fee only when a payment is actually made. The standard fee is 1% of the agreed price. Large transactions pay a lower rate of 0.5%. The exact amounts are shown to both people before the buyer pays. The fee is taken from the seller's side, so the buyer pays exactly the agreed price. The rates may change, and the rates shown before you pay apply to that trade.
  • After a trade is completed, LBN pays the seller's share to the bank account the seller has provided, as soon as reasonably possible. Sellers must give correct bank details.
  • LBN holds payments only as a service to buyers and sellers. LBN is not the seller of the goods or services.

6. Disputes

If something goes wrong, a buyer or seller can open a dispute in the trade chat and add evidence such as photos or screenshots. If the agreed delivery date has passed without delivery, the buyer can report it. The LBN team reads the chat and the evidence, may message both people, and decides: release the money to the seller, refund the buyer, or split the money. While a dispute is open the trade cannot be completed. Our decision settles the matter on LBN. It does not take away any legal rights you have outside LBN. Opening false or abusive disputes can lead to suspension. See the Refunds and Disputes policy.

Ambassador Monitoring Department

Independent of the ambassadors it supervises and accountable to management.

Performance and verification

Points: 1 per verified registration + 2 per active member + 5 per paid subscription − 3 per suspicious referral. Verified means the email is confirmed and the referral is not flagged. Active means signed in within 30 days. Referred members show by first name only, and opening an ambassador's list is recorded.

Field verification and inspections

Reports on school visits, checks of promotional activity and random member contact. A report is private to its officer until submitted, cannot be changed after submitting, and is reviewed by the Head or a Performance Officer. Everything is recorded.

Anti-fraud

Warning signs found automatically among each ambassador's referred members. A warning is a reason to look, not proof. Open a case, record what you find, and close it as fraud confirmed or no fraud. Confirmed fraud alerts the admins.

Monthly performance reports

Generate a draft for every ambassador for a month. Numbers are filled in automatically with suggested achievements and areas to improve. Set targets, edit the text, then publish. Published reports are locked and appear on the ambassador's page. Score = how much of the targets were reached, out of 100.
